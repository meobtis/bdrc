# Bauddha Stotra Saṃgraha

rūpastavaḥsarvabhūtamaṇikampita tubhyaṃkṣāntimeva caritaṃ tava rūpam |sarvarūpavaradivyasurūpaṃtaṃ
namāmi daśabalavararūpam

bhṛṅgarājaśikhimūrdhni sukeśaṃkokilābhaśikhidivyasukeśam |snigdhanīlamṛdukuñcitakeśaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavarakeśam

śaṅkhakundakumudaṃ vimaromaṃjanmaduḥkhavigataṃ vimaromam |rogaśokavimate vimaromaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavararomam

pūrṇacandradyutiśobhitavaktraṃtuṅganāsamaṇibhāśubhavaktram |buddhapaṅkajinabhāmaravaktraṃtaṃ
namāmi daśabalavaravaktram

raśmisahasravicitrasumūrdhnipuṣpavarṣaśatapūjitamūrdhni |ratnavarṣaśatapūjitamūrdhnitaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaramūrdhni

netrapatākavilambitaśīrṣaṃkāñcanachatravaropitaśīrṣam |netravitānasuśobhitaśīrṣaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaraśīrṣam

divyāmbaramukuṭaṃ maṇimukuṭaṃcandraprabhāmukuṭāmaṇimukuṭam |āgataśīrṣamakhaṇḍitamukuṭaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaramukuṭam

cārudalāyatapaṅkajanetraṃdivyajñānavipulāyatanetram |dhyānamokṣaśubhasaṃskṛtanetraṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaranetram

śuddhakarṇaśubhaśuddhasukarṇaṃśuddhaprajñavaraśobhitakarṇam |śuddhajñānavaraśuddhasukarṇaṃtaṃ
namāmi daśabalavarakarṇam

śubhrakundaśaśipaṅkajadantaṃtīkṣṇasulakṣaṇanirmaladantam |viśvapañcadaśapañcasudantaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaradantam

uccavākyasasurāsurajihvāṃdharmajñānakṛtapāpadajihvām |gandhadhūpasasurāsurajihvāṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavarajihvām

meghasudundubhināditaghoṣaṃsatyadharmanayasuviditaghoṣam |divyavādiparavāditaghoṣaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaraghoṣam

grīvagrīvadaśagrīvasugrīvaṃkṣāntivīryaṃ tava grīvasugrīvam |hemavarṇamaṇigrīvasugrīvaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaragrīvam

siṃhakāyahimakundasukāyaṃdhyānakāyaguṇasāgarakāyam |aṣṭasulakṣaṇanirmalakāyaṃtaṃ namāmi daśabalavarakāyam

śvetaraktaśubhaśobhitavastraṃnīlapītaharitāyitavastram |hāradhavalaśubhaśobhitavastraṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaravastram

hemanāgakarasaṃskṛtabāhūpuṣpadānakṛtaśobhitabāhū |dhīravīryasumanoharabāhūtaṃ (tau) namāmi daśabalavarabāhū

cāmaracakrasuśobhitahastaṃvimalasukomalapaṅkajahastam |bhūtapretaśaraṇāgatahastaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavarahastam

rāgadveṣatanuvarjitacittaṃkalpakoṭisamabhāvitacittam |śīlajñānasamabhāvitacittaṃtaṃ namāmi daśabalavaracittam

padmanābhaśubhaśobhitanābhaṃpadmayoniśubhaśobhitanābham |brahmajyotisuśobhitanābhaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaranābham

cakrasulakṣaṇabhūṣitapādaṃaṅkuśaśaktivirājitapādam |nāgayakṣagaṇavanditapādaṃtaṃ namāmi daśabalavarapādam

bhāskaradyutirājitapādaṃdevadānavagaṇavanditapādam |sarvadevagaṇavanditapādaṃtaṃ namāmi daśabalavarapādam

sarvadevagaṇapūjitaśīrṣaṃsūryakoṭisamabhāvitaśīrṣam |divyajvālajvalitārpitaśīrṣaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaraśīrṣam

puṣpadhūpaśatapūjitaśīrṣaṃdīpagandhaśatapūjitaśīrṣam |mālyavastraśatapūjitaśīrṣaṃtaṃ namāmi
daśabalavaraśīrṣam

tribhuvanadharmakalābhyuṣṇīṣa-ratnachatramukuṭādipūjitam |ye paṭhanti sugatastavamenaṃte labhanti
śubhamokṣapathādhigamam

śrīmadāryāvalokiteśvarabhaṭṭārakasya rūpastavaṃ samāptam |

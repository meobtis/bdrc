# Madhyanatavibhagashastram

## Lakṣaṇaparicchedaḥ prathamaḥ

ārya maitreyapraṇītāmadhyāntavibhāgakārikālakṣaṇaparicchedaḥ prathamaḥlakṣaṇaṃ hyāvṛtistattvaṃ
pratipakṣasya bhāvanā|tatrā'vasthā phalaprāptiryānā ''nuttaryameva ca

abhūtaparikalpo'sti dvayaṃ tatra na vidyate|śūnyatā vidyate tvatra tasyāmapi sa vidyate

na śūnyaṃ nā'pi cā'śūnyaṃ tasmāt sarvaṃ vidhīyate|sattvādasattvāt sattvācca madhyamā pratipacca
sā

arthasattvātmavijñaptipratibhāsaṃ prajāyate|vijñānaṃ nāsti cāsyārthastadabhāvāttadapyasat

abhūtaparikalpatvaṃ siddhamasya bhavatyataḥ|na tathā sarvathā'bhāvāt tatkṣayānmuktiriṣyate

kalpitaḥ paratantraśca pariniṣpanna eva ca|arthādabhūtakalpācca dvayā'bhāvācca deśitaḥ

upalabdhiṃ samāśritya nopalabdhiḥ prajāyate|nopalabdhiṃ samāśritya nopalabdhiḥ prajāyate

upalabdhestataḥ siddhā nopalabdhisvabhāvatā|tasmācca samatā jñeyā nopalambhopalambhayoḥ

abhūtaparikalpaśca cittacaittāstridhātukāḥ|tatrārthadṛṣṭirvijñānaṃ tadviśeṣe tu caitasāḥ

evaṃ pratyayavijñānaṃ dvitīyaṃ caupabhogikam|upabhogaparicchedaprerakāstatra caitasāḥ

chādanādropaṇāccaiva nayanātsaṃparigrahāt|pūraṇāt triparicchedādupabhogācca karṣaṇāt

nibandhanādābhimakhyād duḥkhanāt kliśyate jagat|tredhā dvedhā ca saṃkleśaḥ saptadhā'bhūtakalpanāt

lakṣaṇaṃ cā'tha paryāyastadartho bheda eva ca|sādhanañceti vijñeyaṃ śūnyatāyāḥ samāsataḥ

dvayā'bhāvo hyabhāvasya bhāvaḥ śūnyasya lakṣaṇam|na bhāvo nā'pi cā'bhāvao na pṛthaktvaikalakṣaṇam

tathatā bhūtakoṭiścā'nimittaṃ paramārthatā|dharmadhātuśca paryāyāḥ śūnyatāyāḥ samāsataḥ

ananyathā'viparyāsatannirodhāryagocaraiḥ|hetutvāccāryadharmaṇāṃ paryāyārtho yathākramam

saṃkliṣṭā ca viśuddhā ca samalā nirmalā ca sā|abdhātukanakākāśaśuddhivacchuddhiriṣyate

bhoktṛbhojanataddhehapratiṣṭhāvastuśūnyatā|tacca yena yathā dṛṣṭaṃ yadarthaṃ tasya śūnyatā

śubhadvayasya prāptyartha sadā sattvahitāya ca|saṃsārā'tyajanārthaṃ ca kuśalasyā'kṣayāya ca

gotrasya ca viśuddhayarthaṃ lakṣaṇavyañjanāptaye|śuddhaye bu[ddha]dharmāṇāṃ bodhisattvaḥ prapadyate

pudgalasyā'tha dharmāṇāmabhāvaḥ śūnyatā'tra hi|tadabhāvasya sadbhāvastasmin sā śūnyatā'parā

saṃkliṣṭā cedbhavennāsau muktāssyuḥ sarvadehinaḥ|viśuddhā cedbhavennā'sau vyāyāmo niṣphalo bhavet

na kliṣṭā nā'pi va'ākliṣṭā śuddhā'śuddhā na caiva sā|prabhāsvaratvāccittasya kleśasyāgantukatvataḥ

||iti lakṣaṇaparicchedaḥ prathamaḥ||

## Pratipakṣabhāvanā'vasthāphalaparicchedaścaturthaḥ

pratipakṣabhāvanā'vasthāphalaparicchedaścaturthaḥdauṣṭhulyāt tarṣahetutvād vastutvādavimohataḥ|catussatyāvatārāya
smṛtyupasthānabhāvanā

parijñāte vipakṣe ca pratipakṣe ca sarvathā|tadapāyāya vīryaṃ hi caturdhā sampravartate

karmaṇyatā sthitestatra sarvārthānāṃ samṛddhaye|pañcadoṣaprahāṇā'ṣṭasaṃskārāsevanā'nvayā

kausīdyamavavādasya sammoṣo laya uddhavaḥ|asaṃskāro'tha saṃskāraḥ pañca doṣā ime matāḥ

āśrayo'thāśritastasya nimittaṃ phalameva ca|ālambane'sammoṣo layauddhatyānubuddhyanā

tadapāyā'bhisaṃskāraḥ śāntau praśaṭhavāhitā|ropite mokṣabhāgīye cchandayogādhipatyataḥ

ālambane'sammoṣāvisāravicayasya ca|vipakṣasya hi saṃlekhāt pūrvasya phalamuttaram

dvau dvau nirvedhabhāgīyāvindriyāṇi balāni ca|āśrayāṅgaṃ svabhāvāṅgaṃ niryāṇāṅgaṃ tṛtīyakam

caturthamanuśaṃsāṅgaṃ niḥkleśāṅgaṃ tridhā matam|nidānenāśrayeṇeha svabhāvena ca deśitam

paricchedo'tha samprāptiḥ parasambhāvanā tridhā|vipakṣapratipakṣaśca mārgasyāṅgaṃ tadaṣṭadhā

dṛṣṭau śīle'tha saṃlekhe paravijñaptiriṣyate|kleśopakleśavaibhutvavipakṣapratipakṣatā

anukūlā viparyastā sānubandhā viparyayā|aviparyastaviparyāsā'nanubandha ca bhāvanā

ālambanamanaskāraprāptitastad viśiṣṭatā|hetvavasthā'vatārākhyā prayogaphalasaṃjñitā

kāryākāryaviśiṣṭā ca uttarā'nuttarā ca sā|adhimuktau praveśe ca niryāṇe vyākṛtāvapi

kathikatve'bhiṣeke ca samprāptāvanuśaṃsane|kṛtyānuṣṭhāna uddiṣṭā dharmādhātau tridhā punaḥ

aśuddhāśuddhaśuddhā ca viśuddhā ca yathārthataḥ|pudgalānāṃ vyavasthānaṃ yathāyogamato matam

bhājanatvaṃ vipākākhyaṃ balantasyādhipatyataḥ|rucirvṛddhirviśuddhiśca phalametad yathākramam

uttarottaramādyañca tadabhyāsat samāptitaḥ|ānukūlyād vipakṣācca visaṃyogād viśeṣataḥ

uttarā'nuttaratvācca phalamanyat samāsataḥ|||iti pratipakṣabhāvanādiparicchedaścaturthaḥ||

## Tattvaparicchedastṛtīyaḥ

tattvaparicchedastṛtīyaḥmūlalakṣaṇatattvamaviparyāsalakṣaṇam|phalahetumayaṃ tattvaṃ sūkṣmaudārikameva
ca

prasiddhaṃ śuddhiviṣayaṃ saṅgrāhyaṃ bhedalakṣaṇam|kauśalyatattvaṃ daśadhā ātmadṛṣṭivipakṣataḥ

svabhāvastrividho'sacca nityaṃ saccā'pyatattvataḥ|sadasattattvataśceti svabhāvatrayamiṣyate

samāropā'pavādasya dharmapudgalayoriha|grāhyagrāhakayoścāpi bhāvā'bhāve ca darśanam

yajjñānānna pravarteta taddhi tattvasya lakṣaṇam|asadartho hyanityārtha utpādadavyayalakṣaṇaḥ

samalā'malabhāvena mūlatattve yathākramam|duḥkhamādānalakṣmākhyaṃ sambandhenā'paraṃ matam

abhāvaścāpyatadbhāvaḥ prakṛtiḥ śūnyatā matā|alakṣaṇañca nairātmyaṃ tad-vilakṣaṇameva ca

svalakṣaṇañca nirdiṣṭaṃ duḥkhasatyamato matam|vāsanā'tha samutthānamavisaṃyoga eva ca

svabhāvadvayanotpattirmalaśāntidvayaṃ matam|parijñāyāṃ prahāṇe ca prāptisākṣātkṛtāvayam

mārgasatyaṃ samākhyātaṃ prajñaptipratipattitaḥ|tathodbhāvanayaudāraṃ paramārthantu ekataḥ

arthaprāptiprapattyā hi paramārthastridhā mataḥ|nirvikārā'viparyāsapariniṣpattito dvayam

lokaprasiddhamekasmāt trayād yuktiprasiddhakam|viśuddhagocaraṃ dvedhā ekasmādeva kīrtitam

nimittasya vikalpasya nāmnaśca dvayasaṅgrahaḥ|samyagjñānasatattvasya ekenaiva ca saṅgrahaḥ

pravṛttitattvaṃ dvividhaṃ sanniveśakupannatā|ekaṃ lakṣaṇavijñaptiśuddhisamyakprapannatā

ekahetutvabhoktṛtvakartṛtvavaśavartane|ādhipatyārthanityatve kleśaśuddhyāśraye'pi ca

yogitvā'muktamuktatve ātmadarśanameṣu hi|parikalpavikalpārthadharmatārthena teṣu te

anekatvā'bhisaṅkṣepaparicchedārtha āditaḥ|grāhakagrāhyatadgrāhabījārthaścā'paro mataḥ

veditārthaparicchedabhogāyadvārato'param|punarhetuphalāyāsānāropā'napavādataḥ

aniṣṭeṣṭaviśuddhīnāṃ samotpattyādhipatyayoḥ|samprāptisamudācārapāratantryārthato'param

grahaṇasthānasandhānabhogaśuddhidvayārthataḥ|phalahetūpayogārthanopayogāttathā'param

vedanāsanimittārthatannimittaprapattitaḥ|tacchamapratipakṣārthayogādaparamiṣyate

guṇadoṣā'vikalpena jñānena parataḥ svayam|niryāṇādaparaṃ jñeyaṃ saprajñaptisahetukāt

nimittāt praśamāt sārthāt paścimaṃ samudāhṛtam||||iti tattvaparicchedastṛtīyaḥ||

## Yānā'nuttaryaparicchedaḥ pañcamaḥ

yānā'nuttaryaparicchedaḥ pañcamaḥānuttaryaṃprapattau hi punarālambane matam|samudāgama uddiṣṭaṃ
pratipattistu ṣaḍ vidhā

paramā'tha manaskāre anudharme'ntavarjane|viśiṣṭā cāviśiṣṭā ca paramā dvādaśātmikā

audāryamāyatatvañca adhikaro'kṣayātmatā|nairantaryamakṛcchratvaṃ vittatvañca parigrahaḥ

ārambhaprāptiniṣyandaniṣpattiḥ paramā matā|tataśca paramārthena daśa pāramitā matāḥ

dānaṃ śīlaṃ kṣamā vīryaṃ dhyānaṃ prajñā upāyatā|praṇidhānaṃ balaṃ jñānametāḥ pāramitā daśa

anugraho'vighātaśca karma tasya ca marṣaṇam|guṇavṛddhiśca sāmarthyamavatāravimocane

akṣayatvaṃ sadā vṛttirniyataṃ bhogapācane|yathāprajñaptito dharmamahāyānamanaskriyā

bodhisattvasya satataṃ prajñayā triprakārayā|dhātupuṣṭayaipraveśāya cārthasiddhyai bhavatyasau

saṃyuktā dharmacaritaiḥ sā jñeyā daśabhiḥ punaḥ|lekhanā pūjanā dānaṃ śravaṇaṃ vācanod grahaḥ

prakāśanā'tha svādhyāyaścintanā bhāvanā ca tat|ameyapuṇyaskandhaṃ hi caritaṃ tad daśātmakam

viśeṣādakṣayatvācca parānugrahato'śamāt|avikṣiptā'viparyāsapraṇatā cā'nudhārmikī

vyutthānaṃ viṣaye sārastathāsvādalayoddhavaḥ|sambhāvanā'bhisandhiśca manaskāre'pyahaṃkṛtiḥ

hīnacittañca vikṣepaḥ parijñeyo hi dhīmatā|vyañjanā'rthamanaskāre'visāre lakṣaṇadvaye

aśuddhaśuddhāvāgantukatve'trāsitā'nunnatau|saṃyogāt saṃstavāccaiva viyogādapyasaṃstavāt

arthasattvamasattvañca vyañjane so'viparyayaḥ|dvayena pratibhāsatvaṃ tathā cā'vidyamānatā

arthe sa cā'viparyāsaḥ sadasattvena varjitaḥ|tajjalpabhāvito jalpamanaskārastadāśrayaḥ

manaskāre'viparyāso dvayaprakhyānakāraṇe|māyādivadasattvañca sattvañcā'rthasya tanmatam

so'visāre'viparyāso bhāvā'bhāvā'visārataḥ|sarvasya nāmamātratvaṃ sarvakalpā'pravṛttaye

svalakṣaṇe'viparyāsaḥ paramārthe svalakṣaṇe|dharmadhātuvinirmukto yasmād dharmo na vidyate

sāmānyalakṣaṇantasmāt sa ca tatrā'viparyayaḥ|viparyastamanaskārā'vihāniparihāṇitaḥ

tadaśuddhirviśuddhiśca sa ca tatrā'viparyayaḥ|dharmadhātorviśuddhatvāt prakṛtyā vyomavatpunaḥ

dvayasyāgantukatvaṃ hi sa ca tatrā'viparyayaḥ|saṃkleśaśca viśuddhiśca dharmapudgalayorna hi

asattvāt trāsatāmānau nā'taḥ so'trā'viparyayaḥ|pṛthaktvaikatvamantaśca tīrthyaśrāvakayorapi

samāropā'pavādā'nto dvidhā pudgaladharmayoḥ|vipakṣapratipakṣā'ntaḥ śāśvatocchedasaṃjñitaḥ

grāhyagrāhakasaṃkleśavyavadāne dvidhā tridhā|vikalpadvayatā'ntaśca sa ca saptavidho mataḥ

bhāvā'bhāve praśāmye'tha śamane trāsyatadbhaye|grāhyagrāhe'tha samyaktvamithyātve vyāpṛtau na
ca

ajanmasamakālatve sa vikalpadvayā'ntatā|viśiṣṭā cā'viśiṣṭā ca jñeyā daśasu bhūmiṣu

vyavasthānantato dhātuḥ sādhyasādhanadhāraṇā|avadhārapradhārā ca prativedhaḥ pratānatā

pragamaḥ praśaṭhatvañca prakarṣālambanammatam|avaikalyā'pratikṣepo'vikṣepaśca prapūraṇā

samutpādo nirūḍhiśca karmaṇyatvā'pratiṣṭhitā|nirāvaraṇatā tasyā'praśrabdhisamudāgamaḥ

śāstraṃ madhyavibhāgaṃ hi gūḍhasārārthameva ca|mahārthañcaiva sarvārthaṃ sarvā'narthapraṇodanam

||iti yānānuttarya paricchedaḥ pañcamaḥ||samāptā madhyāntavibhāgakārikāḥ

## Āvaraṇaparicchedo dvitīyaḥ

āvaraṇaparicchedo dvitīyaḥvyāpi prādeśikodriktasamādānavivarjanam|dvayāvaraṇamākhyātaṃ navadhā
kleśalakṣaṇam

saṃyojanānyāvaraṇamudvegasamupekṣayoḥ|tattvadṛṣṭeśca satkāyadṛṣṭestadvastuno'pi ca

nirodhamārgaratneṣu lābhasatkāra eva ca|saṅkleśasya parijñāne śubhādau daśadhā'param

aprayogo'nāyatane'yogavihitaśca yaḥ|notpattiramanaskāraḥ sambhārasyā'prapūrṇatā

gotramitrasya vaidhuryaṃ cittasya parikheditā|pratipatteśca vaidhuryaṃ kuduṣṭajanavāsatā

dauṣṭhulyamavaśiṣṭatvaṃ trayāt prajñā'vipakvatā|prakṛtyā caiva dauṣṭhulyaṃ kausīdyaṃ ca pramāditā

saktirbhave ca bhoge ca līnacittatvameva ca|aśraddhā'nadhimuktiśca yathārutavicāraṇā

saddharme'gauravaṃ lābhe gurutā'kṛpatā tathā|śrutavyasanamalpatvaṃ samādhyaparikarmitā

śubhaṃ bodhiḥ samādānaṃ dhīmattvā'bhrāntyanāvṛtī|nṛtyatrāso'matsaritvaṃ vaśitvañca śubhādayaḥ

trīṇi trīṇi ca eteṣāṃ jñeyānyāvaraṇāni hi|pakṣyapāramitābhūmiṣvanyadāvaraṇaṃ punaḥ

vastvakauśalakausīdyaṃ samādherdvayahīnatā|aropaṇā'tha daurbalyaṃ dṛṣṭidauṣṭhulyaduṣṭatā

aiśvaryasyā'tha sugateḥ sattvā'tyāgasya cāvṛtiḥ|hānivṛddhyośca doṣāṇāṃ guṇānāmavatāraṇe

vimocane'kṣayatve ca nairantarye śubhasya ca|niyatīkaraṇe dharmasambhogaparipācane

sarvatragārthe agrārthe niṣyandāgrārtha eva ca|niṣparigrahatārtha ca santānā'bheda eva ca

nissaṅkleśaviśuddhyarthe'nānātvārtha eva ca|ahīnā'nadhikārthe ca caturdhāvaśitāśraye

dharmadhātāvavidyeyamakliṣṭā daśadhāvṛtiḥ|daśabhūmivipakṣeṇa pratipakṣāstu bhūmayaḥ

kleśāvaraṇamākhyātaṃ jñeyāvaraṇameva ca|sarvāṇyāvaraṇānīha yatkṣayānmuktiriṣyate

||ityāvaraṇaparicchedo dvitīyaḥ||

## §1-20

| chos kyi sde dpon khyab bdag dpal || śes rab chen po śā ri'i bus || 'jig rten rnams la snyiṅ

| de tshe dzam bu'i gliṅ 'di yaṅ || chu źeṅ tha gru mnyam par ni || dpag tshad stoṅ phrag bcu

## §21-40

| de ni bltams ma thag tu yaṅ || gom pa bdun dag (492a1) 'dor bar 'gyur || gom pa re rer gter

| yaṅ daṅ yaṅ du phyir mi 'oṅ || zag med mya ṅan 'das 'gro źes || gsuṅ yaṅ rab tu bka' stsal
to || klu rnams kyis ni chu (3)yi rgyun || bsil daṅ 'jam pas sku bsil 'gyur |[36]

| nam mkha'i ṅos nas lha rdzas kyi || gos daṅ me tog 'bab par 'gyur || de yi dbu la gdugs dkar
po || lha (4)rnams kyis ni 'dzin par 'gyur |[37]

| mtshan mchog sum cu gnyis mṅa' ba || byams pa dpal gyis 'bar ba de || ma mas yaṅ dag blaṅs
nas su || yum gyi (5)phyag tu 'bul bar 'gyur |[38]

| rin chen sna tshogs kyis brgyan pa'i || khyogs ni yid du 'oṅ ba la || sras daṅ bcas par bźugs

## §61-80

| śākya seṅge'i bstan pa la || bslab (6)gźi yaṅ dag blaṅs nas su || ji ltar gsuṅ bźin bsgrubs

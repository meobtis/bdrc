# Bodhisatvacaryānirdeśa

## Colophon

’phags pa byaṅ chub sems dpa’i spyod pa bstan pa źes bya ba theg pa chen po’i mdo rdzogs so
|| || rgya gar gyi mkhan po dzi na mi tra daṅ | pradzñā varma daṅ | źu chen gyi lotstsha ba
pan de ye śes sde la sogs pas bsgyur ciṅ źus te gtan la phab pa’o ||

saṅs rgyas daṅ byaṅ chub sems dpa’ thams cad la phyag ’tshal lo ||
